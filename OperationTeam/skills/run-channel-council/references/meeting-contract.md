# Channel council contract

## Cadence

- Standard: every Monday at 09:00 Asia/Tokyo.
- Evidence window: latest 7 complete days compared with the prior 7 days where possible.
- Also compare individual videos at equivalent ages such as 24 and 72 hours.
- Monthly: use the first Monday meeting to add a broader format and business-readiness review.

## Agenda

1. Data freshness and missing fields
2. Previous actions and whether their decision points were reached
3. Channel and video performance
4. Content and production interpretation
5. Planning implications
6. Business-readiness signal
7. Challenge round and disagreements
8. Decisions, next actions, and approval requests

## Decision rules

- Do not call a format successful from one low-view video.
- Do not compare cumulative views without publication age.
- Treat Shorts-feed exposure, average percentage viewed, viewed-versus-swiped, search traffic, reactions, and subscriber conversion as different signals.
- Keep experiments attributable: change at most three major variables, preferably one.
- Preserve channel vision while testing formats.
- BusinessTeam may prepare an opportunity but external activity remains human-gated.
- An empty or failed data source is itself a recorded finding, not permission to estimate.
