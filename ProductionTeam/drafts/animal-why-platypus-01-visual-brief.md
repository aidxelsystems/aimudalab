# 「この体、何のため？」カモノハシ編 — visual brief

## 制作意図

- 三択は入口、正解後の仕組み説明を本編にする。
- 0秒からカモノハシの顔と大きなくちばしを見せる。
- 不可視の微弱電気は、必ず`AIイメージ`表示と組み合わせる。
- 4枚の基礎画像を、寄り・引き・暗転・選択肢・正解スタンプ・説明オーバーレイで14視覚ビートへ展開する。

## asset 01：hook-face

Use case: photorealistic-natural. 9:16 Shorts hero frame. Underwater extreme close-up of a scientifically recognizable platypus swimming toward camera, eyes visibly closed, broad rubbery bill dominating the center, wet dark brown fur, expressive and slightly surprising face, realistic anatomy, documentary wildlife photography, suspended particles and dramatic blue-green light. Reserve upper 35% as simple dark water negative space. Main face and bill in middle 35–75%. Bottom 20% free of critical details. No text, logos, fantasy glow, extra limbs, or cartoon style.

## asset 02：closed-senses

Use case: scientific-educational. Photorealistic side-profile underwater platypus foraging along a river bottom, eyes closed, ear grooves and nostrils visibly sealed, broad bill sweeping near gravel, realistic webbed feet and fur, credible Australian freshwater environment. Upper 35% calm dark water for captions, animal centered in middle, bottom 20% no critical details. No text, labels, logos, fantasy effects, or inaccurate anatomy.

## asset 03：bill-and-prey

Use case: scientific-educational. Photorealistic underwater macro scene of a platypus bill approaching a small freshwater shrimp above river gravel. Show only very subtle thin cyan pulse lines around the moving shrimp traveling toward the bill as an educational visualization of weak electrical and pressure cues, not lightning and not visible magic. The platypus remains anatomically accurate. Upper 35% clean dark water. Main bill and shrimp middle 35–75%. Bottom 20% free of critical details. No text or logos.

## asset 04：electric-footprints

Use case: stylized-concept grounded in science. Cinematic photorealistic underwater platypus following a trail of delicate dotted cyan ripples left by a swimming shrimp, visual metaphor for sensing tiny electrical and pressure changes. Keep the animal natural and realistic; the only stylization is the restrained information trail. Strong face and bill, dark mysterious river, joyful discovery rather than sci-fi combat. Upper 35% simple dark water for captions, subject middle, bottom 20% clear. No text, logos, lightning bolts, machinery, goggles, or anthropomorphic clothing.

## 表示文

- hook: `まさか…\n目・耳・鼻を閉じて\n狩る!?`
- question: `どうやって\n獲物を見つける？`
- choices: `A 超音波` / `B くちばしで電気` / `C 水の味`
- reveal: `正解は B！`
- knowledge 1: `水中では\n目・耳・鼻を\n閉じる`
- knowledge 2: `くちばしで\n圧力と微弱な電気を\n感じ取る`
- AI visualization: `AIイメージ\n電気の足あとを\n読む世界`
- outro: `このくちばし、\n電気を読む“手”だった\n\nチャンネル登録も\nよろしく！`

## 音・リズム

- hook: ドーン＋短い白フラッシュ。顔を白飛びさせない。
- choices: A/B/Cを同時に見せ、1つずつ色を点灯。
- reveal: クイズ正解音。
- knowledge: 効果音を抑え、声と図解を優先。
- AI visualization: シャキーン。
- outro: 冒頭画像へ戻し、ループ感を作る。
