## Reusable pattern

1. AI orbit footage establishes isolation and authority.
2. A primary-source M100 image proves the visible failure.
3. Remotion measurement graphics turn `1.3 mm` into the main impact.
4. AI repair footage supplies human action without claiming documentary accuracy.
5. The official before/after image proves recovery.
6. The technical correction becomes the human-scale phrase `宇宙のメガネ`.
7. A full-screen punchline and CTA close the episode.

## Accuracy lesson

Do not say that the Hubble mirror moved by 1.3 mm. The primary mirror had the wrong curve; the 1.3 mm error was in the spacing of a lens in the null corrector used to test the mirror. Keep symptom, defective part, root cause, and correction separate in future episodes.

## Media lesson

Label AI footage `AI VISUALIZATION`. Label the official M100 image separately and credit `ESA/Hubble & NASA`. This lets emotional reconstruction and factual evidence coexist without confusing the viewer.
