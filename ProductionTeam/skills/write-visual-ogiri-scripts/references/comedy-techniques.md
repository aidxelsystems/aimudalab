# Comedy Techniques

## Core mechanisms

- `あるある`: Build trust through recognizable friction. Mix observation textures.
- `ギャップ`: Contrast expectation and reality, price and value, or tiny cause and huge result.
- `裏切り`: Lead toward the normal interpretation, then reveal a different specialty, motive, or destination.
- `自虐`: Let the customer's vague request, vanity, mistake, or weakness cause the result.
- `天丼`: Repeat a recognized frame with controlled variation.
- `三段落ち`: Make the third similar beat stronger through a category or scale shift.
- `伏線回収`: Plant an unreasonable-looking behavior, then reveal a credible hidden reason that raises the person or place in the viewer's estimation.
- `視点反転`: Move agency to staff, tools, UI, signs, receipts, doors, or the building.
- `静かな狂気`: Let nobody react to the impossible situation.
- `ダジャレ`: Use at most one unless wordplay is the chosen quality; it must work aloud.

## Construction rules

- Give each non-relatable answer a named mechanism. Random impossibility is not enough.
- Let one technique drive structure, one drive rhythm, and optionally one drive the ending.
- Build total escalation; not every line needs to be the strongest line.
- Use a weak beat only when the pause and recovery are designed.
- Use callbacks to reward memory, not to explain earlier jokes.
- Let the flip express the viewer's discovery or add a second interpretation.

## 評価反転型の伏線回収

Use this emotional transition:

```text
setup: 「それはないだろ」「なんでそんなことしてるの」
clue: the odd behavior returns or a second concrete clue appears
payoff: 「そういう理由だったのか」「いや、すげー人じゃん」
```

Require all of the following:

1. The setup is visibly odd and understandable before the explanation.
2. The payoff supplies a causal reason, not merely the same object or phrase.
3. The reason is surprising but logically compatible with the setup.
4. The reveal improves the viewer's evaluation of the person, place, or act through skill, duty, kindness, sacrifice, courage, or hidden expertise.
5. The final image can show evidence of that value rather than relying only on narration.

Classify weaker patterns correctly:

- Same element appears again without new meaning: `天丼` or `反復`.
- Hidden reason is explained but nobody's evaluation changes: `種明かし`.
- The reveal only makes the behavior stranger: `エスカレーション`.
- The reveal makes an earlier bad-looking act admirable: `伏線回収`.

Example:

```text
setup: 女将のあいさつが、なぜか敬礼
clue: 女将の夜の見回りが、館内ではなく街全体
payoff: 女将は元府警の幹部で、今も旅館から街を守っている
emotion: それはないだろ → いや、すげー人じゃん
```

Do not accept poetic repetition such as a mountain returning a bow as伏線回収 unless the repetition also reveals a causal reason and creates a meaningful evaluation change.

## Diagnostic questions

For a weak joke, ask:

1. What was the expected normal image?
2. What single visible fact breaks that expectation?
3. Which mechanism creates the laugh?
4. Does the flip add a second beat?
5. Is it too similar to another slot?
6. Can it become a setup, tendon, recovery, or callback instead of a standalone peak?
7. If it is called a callback, what exact evaluation changes before and after the reveal?

## 伏線の接続文

When setup and clue are separated by several answers, repeat the subject in the clue. Do not write `夜の見回り` if the intended memory is `女将`; write `女将の夜の見回り`. The repeated subject is not redundant—it reconnects the viewer to the planted oddity before the payoff.
