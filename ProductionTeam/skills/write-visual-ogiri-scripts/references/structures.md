# Episode Structures

## Selection rule

Choose one primary structure. Borrow at most one secondary structural device. Change the dominant structure or joke quality from recent episodes when possible.

## 起承転結・感情設計型

```text
a01-a04 起: different flavors of relatable reality
a05-a08 承: visually impossible turn
a09-a11 転: short rush, tendon, callback, or sound pattern
a12-a14 結: payoff and "嫌だけど少し好き"
```

Use broad `それある`, tiny `細かいな`, awkward `気まずい`, nostalgic `昭和っぽい`, and comment-inviting `自分だけかも` rather than four identical relatable observations. A typical title reset is after a04, a08, and a11.

## あるある坂道型

Begin with three familiar frictions, reveal a strange local rule, involve the customer in it, and expose the world's true rule at a14.

## 天丼回収型

Plant a concrete motif, repeat it with variation, let it collide with another joke, then make it act or speak in the peak. Use repetition for recognition, not filler.

Do not label motif repetition as伏線回収. Use `天丼回収` when recognition is the reward. Use `伏線回収` only when the later reason changes `それはないだろ` into `いや、すげー人じゃん`.

## 天丼からの三段落ち

Repeat the same setup or interaction once so the audience learns the frame. On the third beat, change category, scale, status, or destination. Do not call simple repetition `かぶせ`; reserve `かぶせ` for comparative stacking such as “俺はこうだ” followed by “俺のほうがもっとこうだ”.

## 誤解エスカレーター型

Start from one small misunderstanding. Every attempted correction worsens it, the system records it as truth, and the final beat makes it official.

## 画像ボケ→ツッコミ畳みかけ型

```text
a01-a03: relatable entry
a04-a06: clear undesirable exaggeration
a07: short weak joke or pun, then pause
a08-a11: image first, discovery beat, flip/voice tsukkomi
a12-a14: recovery, callback, odd charm
```

Use `visual_pre_roll` around 0.55-0.75 seconds for the image-led block.

## Other useful primaries

- `ツッコミ不在型`: Everybody calmly accepts the abnormal rule.
- `取扱説明書型`: Each answer reveals a rule, exception, or penalty.
- `視点ずらし型`: Shift from customer to staff, object, UI, building, or system.
- `ランキング上昇型`: Escalate visibly through levels to a final boss.
- `伏線二本型`: Plant two clear motifs and combine them near the end; use sparingly.
- `店長・黒幕回収型`: An odd person introduced early becomes the source of hidden care or expertise.
- `無駄に壮大型`: Treat a tiny inconvenience as ceremony, law, security, or disaster.
- `弱いネタ救済型`: Intentionally place one short weak beat, then make the self-aware recovery the laugh.

## Title-call placement

Treat the title call as a reset, not decoration.

- Use an early reset after a03-a04 to launch absurdity.
- Use a middle reset after a06-a08 to mark a rule change.
- Use a reset after a09-a11 before a rush or final block.
- Use one reset only when continuity is strong.
- Use three resets only when repetition itself increases impact.

Record the placement and reason in `_notes` when practical.

## Ending patterns

Prefer one of:

- an early odd detail becomes useful;
- the terrible service reveals excessive care;
- the customer adapts and benefits in an embarrassing way;
- two motifs combine into one visual payoff;
- the place is still undesirable but now slightly tempting.

For a person-centered伏線回収 ending, verify this chain:

```text
odd action → second observable clue → hidden background or duty → admirable present action
```

The background alone is insufficient. Show what the person is doing now with that skill or responsibility.
